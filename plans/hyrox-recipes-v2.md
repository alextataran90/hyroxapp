# Hyrox Nutrition Plan: Recipe Library

Simple recipes for hybrid / Hyrox training (6-7 sessions per week, evening training at 18:00).
All ingredients from Mercadona (Spain). Pick one recipe per slot each day.

## Daily targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~2,400 kcal |
| Protein | 180-200 g |
| Carbs | 250-300 g |
| Fat | ~50-65 g |
| Eating window | 09:30 to 21:00 |

## Daily slots

| Slot | Time | Code prefix | Target per slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | 09:30-10:00 | BRK | ~500-550 kcal, ~40 g protein |
| Lunch | 13:30-14:00 | LUN | ~600-650 kcal, ~55 g protein |
| Pre-gym snack | 16:30-17:00 | PRE | ~280-320 kcal, carbs + some protein, sweet |
| Training | 18:00 | | |
| Post-gym snack | 19:30 | POST | ~240-280 kcal, ~35 g protein, sweet |
| Dinner | 21:00 | DIN | ~600-650 kcal, ~55 g protein |
| Treat | after dinner | TRT | ~100-120 kcal |

Picking one recipe per slot lands at about 2,300 to 2,600 kcal and 180 to 215 g protein depending on your picks. Lunch and dinner recipes are interchangeable.

## General rules

- Weigh meat raw, potatoes peeled, pasta dry, rice as packed (vasitos).
- Macros are estimates from standard nutrition values. Hacendado labels may differ slightly (especially yogur +Proteínas, barritas, burger meat and vasitos).
- Spices are free (no need to log): sal, pimienta, ajo en polvo, pimentón, orégano, comino, curry, finas hierbas, canela, limón.
- "Fiambre de pavo 98%" = pechuga de pavo or pollo en lonchas with 96-98% meat.
- If daily protein goes over 210 g, reduce meat in lunch or dinner from 200 g to 180 g.

## Cooking times (lunch and dinner)

| Meat | Air fryer (200°C) | Oven (200°C) | Pan (medium-high) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pechuga de pollo (filetes) | 12-14 min, flip halfway | 18-20 min | 4-5 min per side |
| Contramuslo de pollo deshuesado | 14-16 min, flip halfway | 22-25 min | 5-6 min per side |
| Pechuga / solomillo de pavo | 12-14 min, flip halfway | 18-20 min | 4-5 min per side |
| Cinta de lomo de cerdo | 10-12 min, flip halfway | 15-18 min | 3-4 min per side |
| Filetes de ternera | 6-8 min | not ideal | 1-2 min per side |
| Merluza (lomos) | 10-12 min | 15 min | 3-4 min per side |

Chicken and turkey are done when there is no pink inside.

## Spice mixes (per 200 g meat, about ½ tsp each)

| Mix | Spices |
|---|---|
| Classic | sal, pimienta, ajo en polvo |
| Spanish | sal, pimentón de la Vera, ajo en polvo, orégano |
| Lemon herb | sal, pimienta, finas hierbas, a squeeze of limón |
| Mexican | sal, comino, pimentón, ajo en polvo, pinch of guindilla |
| Curry | sal, curry en polvo, ajo en polvo |

# Prep tips

- **Batch cook:** Sunday and Wednesday, air fry or roast 3 days of chicken and turkey. Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
- **One tray:** potatoes + meat + frozen veg on one tray at 200°C, 20 min oven or 15 min air fryer.
- **Swaps:** any frozen veg for another, any carb side for another (1½ vasitos rice = 400 g potatoes = 80 g dry pasta = 200 g gnocchi = 350 g boniato). Macros stay within about 30 kcal.
